# Home (tabs/index)

Scope: the Home tab, first surface of the redesign; its world becomes the app's world. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a board owner, just before stepping on, often in low light; sees today's practice and starts it in one tap, glances at the growing plant and the week.
Constraints: native tab bar stays (Liquid Glass iOS, frosted web lens). Logo (ring + centre nail, "gvozdi" wordmark) stays as is. User-ordered cards (today / stats / week) and Customize sheet, program invite, consent card, install hint all keep working. EN/UK/RU, light and dark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your practice is a living specimen mounted on Home, labelled like a herbarium sheet. Refuses the cream-ground, serif-headline, terracotta wellness card stack the app had.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white mount board (#F4F5F0 sheets on a #E9ECE5 ground), specimen green (#3F6A3A) for growth, progress and the primary action, label ink #22261F, ochre (#B8892B) only for the soil line and earned marks. Golos Text everywhere, weight and size carry hierarchy. Plain rounded sheets, no borders or shadows. Dark: night mount (deep green-black sheets), pale leaf ink.

STORY: The visitor sees the plant grown from their sessions and the streak, knows today's suggested minutes, and taps Begin. The week strip says which days were practised without guilt.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo top left, burger right. The specimen sheet (aspect 1.2) holds the streak numeral and caption top left and the plant, at 0.8 of the sheet width, drawn right of centre on an ochre soil line. No milestone line, no label: the plant is the progress. Below: today's sheet (program line, minutes, 21 program dashes, full-width green Begin). Below that: week sheet with leaf marks (filled = practised, outline = rest).

FORM: Herbarium sheet, safer register, 1st of my safer lineup (grounded), seed: 69b231947ad4f0ee.

Signature move: the plant itself, one shared scale for every stage so growth is visible (2026-10-31: the typed label and "Next" line were removed at the user's request; the tree took their room). Motion: the plant draws its new growth once after a save; Reduce Motion shows it static.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
